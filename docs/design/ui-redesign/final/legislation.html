Part X — Unfair dismissal

Sections 94 to 134

Text as at 1 Oct 2026

Part X gives an employee the right not to be unfairly dismissed and sets out when a dismissal is fair, how the effective date of termination is found, and the remedies a tribunal may order. The text below is the version in force in England and Wales on 1 Oct 2026; later amendments are not incorporated.
