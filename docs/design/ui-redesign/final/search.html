Secretary of State for Business and Trade v Mercer

[2024] UKSC 12·UK Supreme Court·17 Apr 2024

The Supreme Court allowed the appeal and declared s 146 of the Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992 incompatible with Article 11 ECHR insofar as it fails to protect workers against detriment short of dismissal for taking part in lawful strike action. The case is about detriment, not the unfair dismissal regime.
