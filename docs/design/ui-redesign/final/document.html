Supply Agreement

This Agreement is made between Northgate Foods Limited, a company registered in England and Wales with company number 10293847, whose registered office is at 4 Kingsway, Leeds LS1 4DL (“the Buyer”), and Brightwater Packaging Limited, a company registered in England and Wales with company number 09384756, whose registered office is at Unit 7, Brackmills Industrial Estate, Northampton NN4 7DY (“the Supplier”).

1. Definitions and interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, “Charges” means the sums payable by the Buyer for the Products, “Contract Year” means each period of twelve months beginning on the date of this Agreement, and “Products” means the packaging materials listed in Schedule 1.

1.2 Clause headings are for convenience only and do not affect the interpretation of this Agreement.

2. Supply

2.1 The Supplier shall supply the Products to the Buyer in accordance with each Order accepted by the Supplier.

2.2 Each Order shall be placed at least 30 days before the requested delivery date and shall specify the quantities, the delivery address and the date by which delivery is required.

4. Volumes

4.1 The Supplier shall supply the Products in accordance with each Order.

4.2 The Buyer shall order, and the Supplier shall supply, not less than 60,000 45,000 units of the Products in each calendar month during the Term.

4.3 Where the Supplier is unable to supply the minimum volume in any month, it shall notify the Buyer in writing not less than 30 days before the start of that month.

12. Liability

12.1 Neither party limits its liability for death or personal injury caused by its negligence, or for fraud.

12.2 Subject to clause 12.1, each party’s total liability in any Contract Year shall not exceed the Charges paid in that Contract Year paid in the preceding 12 months.1

21. Variation

21.1 No variation of this Agreement shall be effective unless it is in writing and signed by or on behalf of each of the parties.

22. Governing law

22.1 This Agreement and any dispute arising out of it shall be governed by the law of England and Wales.
