Witness statement of Priya Shah

Between Northgate Foods Limited (Claimant) and Brightwater Packaging Limited (Defendant)

I, Priya Shah, of 14 Elmfield Avenue, Leicester LE2 1RB, date of birth 9 May 1988, will say as follows.

I am the Procurement Manager at Northgate Foods Limited. My work email is priya.shah@northgatefoods.example and my mobile number is 07700 900 482.

On 3 March 2026 I received a call from Tom Adebayo of Brightwater Packaging Limited. Mr Adebayo called from 0116 496 0731 and said that Brightwater would only supply 40,000 units a month from April.

On 4 March 2026 I emailed our finance manager, Daniel Okonjo, at daniel.okonjo@northgatefoods.example.

The delivery address was Unit 7, Troon Way Business Centre, Leicester LE4 9HA, and the depot telephone is 0116 496 0744.

On 11 April 2026 I wrote to Brightwater’s solicitors, Harlow & Vance, about invoice BW-4471.

I did not agree to any change. I told Mr Adebayo that any variation would have to be in writing and signed, as clause 21 of the Supply Agreement requires. I made a note of the call the same day, which I exhibit as PS1.

On 10 March 2026 Northgate paid invoice BW-2291 in full by bank transfer from account 41029384, sort code 20-45-77, at the original contract volume.

I exhibit at PS2 a copy of the email chain with Mr Adebayo dated 3 and 4 March 2026, and at PS3 the purchase order for April 2026.

I believe that the facts stated in this witness statement are true. I understand that proceedings for contempt of court may be brought against anyone who makes, or causes to be made, a false statement in a document verified by a statement of truth without an honest belief in its truth.
