Supply Agreement

This Agreement is made between Northgate Foods Limited (company number 10293847) (“the Buyer”) and Brightwater Packaging Limited (company number 09384756) (“the Supplier”).

4. Volumes

4.1 The Supplier shall supply the Products in accordance with each Order.

4.2 The Buyer shall order, and the Supplier shall supply, not less than 60,000 45,000 units of the Products in each calendar month during the Term.12

4.3 Where the Supplier is unable to supply the minimum volume in any month, it shall notify the Buyer in writing not less than 30 days before the start of that month.

11. Delivery

11.1 The Supplier shall deliver the Products to the address specified in each Order.

11.4 The Supplier shall use its reasonable endeavours acting reasonably to deliver each Order by the requested date.3

12. Liability

12.1 Neither party limits its liability for death or personal injury caused by its negligence, or for fraud.

12.2 Subject to clause 12.1, each party’s total liability in any Contract Year shall not exceed the Charges paid in that Contract Year paid in the preceding 12 months.45

18. Confidentiality

18.1 Each party shall keep confidential the terms of this Agreement and any information marked as confidential.

18.2 The Supplier shall not disclose any confidential information to any third party except as required by law or with the Buyer’s prior written consent.6 Moved to 19.4

18.3 The obligations in this clause survive termination for a period of five years.

19. Term and termination

19.1 This Agreement begins on the date above and continues until terminated in accordance with this clause.

19.4 The Supplier shall not disclose any confidential information to any third party except as required by law or with the Buyer’s prior written consent. Moved from 18.2

21. Variation

21.1 No variation of this Agreement shall be effective unless it is in writing and signed by or on behalf of each of the parties.

22. Governing law

22.1 This Agreement and any dispute arising out of it shall be governed by the law of England and Wales.
